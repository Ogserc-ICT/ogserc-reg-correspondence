# OGSERC Registry: guide for registry staff

The Registry system records everything the Commission receives (inward) or sends (outward). It issues acknowledgement slips, including those required by paragraph 10(3) of the OGSERC Order, and emails the acknowledgement to the unit. The Secretary to the Commission or the Head of Administration routes each item. The system chases responses and shows where every file is. It replaces the Registry Correspondence Workbook.

## Signing in

- Go to the Registry address your administrator gives you and sign in with your work email.
- The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password: at least 12 characters, and a short sentence works well.
- After 5 wrong passwords your account locks for 15 minutes. An administrator can unlock it sooner.

## How an item moves

1. **Front desk.** A document handed in is logged at the front desk and gets an intake number (`OGSERC/FD/2026/00001`). No acknowledgement is issued yet.
2. **Registry.** The Registry registers it on *Log inward*. It gets its acknowledgement number, and the slip is printed. The suggested unit is emailed (if the acknowledgement email is switched on).
3. **Routing.** The Secretary to the Commission or the Head of Administration routes it on the *Routing Desk*. They set the unit, file, action, priority, action officer and any deadline.
4. **Forwarding.** The Registry forwards it to the unit and records that.
5. **Closing.** The reply is logged as an outward item and closes it, or the Registry closes it with a note.

## The Front Desk (front desk officers)

- After signing in you land on the **Front Desk**. Fill in the date and time received (it starts at now), the mode (hand delivery unless you change it), the sender and the subject, then press **Save to front desk log**. Add the type if you know it, who delivered it, a phone number, how many files or volumes, and any remarks.
- The message gives the intake number: *Logged as OGSERC/FD/2026/00001. No acknowledgement has been issued…* Pass the document to the Registry.
- The **Awaiting registry** list below the form shows what the Registry hasn't registered yet, and for how many working days.
- **Front Desk Log** lists the documents you've logged and the acknowledgement number each was registered under.

## The Registry Desk

The home page shows today's figures:
- received today and dispatched today
- awaiting routing / forwarding, and incoming overdue
- not yet dispatched and replies overdue to us
- files out
- awaiting routing, and awaiting registry at the front desk

Click a figure to see the items behind it. The buttons start **Log inward** and **Log outward**. **Front Desk** and **Front Desk Log** show intakes. **Route an item** is for routers only. **Resend ack email** sends an acknowledgement again. You can also print a slip for any inward number. A notice appears when acknowledgement emails are waiting for review.

## Logging an incoming item

1. **Start from the front desk, if it came that way.** On *Log inward*, pick the document under *Front desk intake* and press **Pull details**, or press **Register** next to it on the Front Desk. The receipt time, mode, type, sender, subject, phone, deliverer, volumes and remarks are filled in from the front desk. If you had already typed something, you're asked before it's replaced. The front desk's receipt time is kept because it drives the para 10(3) date. If you must change the time or mode, give a reason; the change is recorded.
2. **Record receipt.** Otherwise, enter the date and time received (it starts at now), the mode, the type, the sender and sender category, and the subject. You appear as "Received by". For updates, annual confirmations and deficiency responses, enter the sender's existing OGSERC number. If the item answers one of our letters, find our outward number under *In response to our ref.*: type part of the number or name and choose it.
3. **Note the contents.** For registration filings, count the Schedule 2 documents (12 required; 13 for a multi-State licensee), say whether fee evidence is attached, and record the payment reference and amount.
4. **Save.** Choose **Save to register**, **Save + print slip** or **Save + PDF slip**. The acknowledgement number is given when you save and never changes. The message tells you the suggested unit and what happened to the email:
   *Saved as OGSERC/ACK/2026/00012.*
   *Suggested unit: Licensing unit*
   *Routing, file reference and handling will be set by the Secretary / Head of Admin on the Routing Desk.*
5. **Give the slip.** Print it, sign and stamp it, give the top copy to the sender and file the Registry copy. For email or portal items, send the PDF. Record how it went under *Acknowledgement via*.

You don't choose the unit, file, action, priority or deadline on this form any more: the item shows **Awaiting routing** until it's routed. If you leave out something required, the form lists every missing field under "Please complete:" and keeps what you typed.

## Routing (Secretary to the Commission and Head of Administration)

- Open the **Routing Desk**, type part of the inward number, sender or subject, choose the item and press **Load**.
- You see the sender, subject, type, status, current unit and division. Set the **unit** (only to override the type's unit), **file reference**, **action required**, **priority**, **action officer** and **response due** (only when the sender sets a deadline). Then press **Save routing**.
- If the item answers one of our letters and has no file yet, that letter's file is already chosen for you, with a note saying so. Check it and change it if needed.
- The message confirms it: *OGSERC/ACK/2026/00012 routed to Licensing unit by …*, with the new status. If the item moved to a unit that hasn't had the acknowledgement yet, that unit is emailed. If the Commission has switched it on, the unit that had the acknowledgement is told the item has moved, and that no action is needed from them.
- You can route an item again. Every routing is kept in the **Routing history** beside the form. Closed and void items can't be routed.
- Only people your administrator has marked **Can route** see the Routing Desk. Routing gives no other access: it doesn't open confidential or personnel files.

## Handling an item (Registry)

- **Forward it.** On the item's page, press **Forwarded today** when it goes to the unit, or enter the date and who received it under *Update item*. If it hasn't been routed yet, you're asked *…has not been routed by the Secretary / Head of Admin yet. Forward it anyway?* If you go ahead, the item is flagged **Forwarded before routing**.
- **Close it.** Log the Commission's reply as an outward item with this inward number under *In reply to*. The inward item closes when the reply is **dispatched**. A holding reply ("Acknowledgement / holding reply") doesn't close it. If the matter is settled another way, press **Close today** and write a short closing note.
- *Update item* now changes only the forwarding date, who received it in the unit, how the acknowledgement was issued, the date closed and the remarks. Everything else about handling is set on the Routing Desk.

## Acknowledgement emails

An administrator chooses how they work (Admin › Settings):
- **Off:** nothing is emailed.
- **Review:** each email waits on the **Emails** page under *Emails to review*. Read it, then press **Send** or **Discard**.
- **Send:** emailed when the item is saved and when it's routed to a new unit.

Emails go to the unit's and its division's addresses (Admin › Organisation). If neither has one, the message says so. An email that couldn't be sent is tried again daily and shown in the email log. To send one again, press **Resend ack email** on the item's page, or enter the number under *Resend an acknowledgement* on the Emails page. If it was emailed before, you're asked first.

## Logging an outgoing item

1. **Number it first.** Before the letter is signed, enter the date of the document, type, subject, addressee, recipient category and originating unit. Choose who signs it from the list; their designation fills in. The Secretary to the Commission is chosen to start with. For someone not on the list, choose *Someone not on the list*, then type the name and designation. Press **Save & get number** and quote the number shown on the letter.
2. **Link it.** Choose the inward item it replies to (check that the sender shown is right) and the file.
3. **Dispatch it.** Press **Dispatched today** on the item's page, or enter the date, mode and tracking reference. When delivery is confirmed, press **Delivered today**. The system won't record a delivery before the dispatch.
4. **Follow up.** Where a reply is expected (deficiency notices, information requests, fee demands and so on), the response due date is set from the dispatch date. The item shows *Response received* as soon as the reply is logged as an inward item quoting this number.

## Files (Internal File Desk)

Files move only between OGSERC units and officers.
- **Issue a file:** open it and press **Issue file**, with who it goes to, **which unit** (required, chosen from the list) and why. If it's still out with someone else, you'll be asked to confirm before it's issued again.
- **Record a return:** press **Record return** when it comes back.
- **Add a new file:** choose the file class and leave the reference blank; the form shows the reference it will get, e.g. *OGSERC/RL/002*. You may type a reference instead; it must not already exist (capitals don't matter).

The File Index shows who has each file and for how long. Files overdue for return are shown in red.

## End of the day and the week

- **Daily Register:** print it at the end of each day, check the received and dispatched counts against the physical items and the dispatch book, and sign it with the Head of Registry. It lists items by the day they actually arrived. An email at 18:40 is on today's register even though it counts as received tomorrow.
- **Pending Actions:** review it with each unit weekly. It shows everything still open, overdue, awaiting routing or awaiting forwarding; print it for all units or one.

## Numbers and dates

| | |
|---|---|
| Front desk intake no. | `OGSERC/FD/YYYY/00001`. YYYY is the year it was handed in (not the deemed year) |
| Inward / Ack. No. | `OGSERC/ACK/YYYY/00001`. YYYY is the year of the deemed receipt date; the serial restarts each year |
| Outward No. | `OGSERC/OUT/YYYY/00001`. YYYY is the year of the document date |
| File reference | `OGSERC/{class code}/001`, the next number for that file class |
| Deemed receipt (para 10(3)) | Email or portal items at or after the closing time (5:00 p.m.), and any item on a weekend or public holiday, count as received on the next working day. That date drives the registration deadline check and every response target |
| Targets | All in working days, skipping public holidays |

## Colours

The status is always written in words. The colour is a reminder:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Overdue |
| Amber | Awaiting action (routing, forwarding, registry) |
| Green | Closed / done |
| Peach | Flagged: something to check, listed on the item |

## What's different from the workbook

- **Numbers never change and nothing is deleted.** An item logged by mistake is *voided* by the Head of Registry with a reason. It stays on the register, struck through, and its number is never used again.
- **No more "Paste > Values".** Numbers are fixed the moment you save.
- **Links are chosen, not typed.** You can't link to a number that doesn't exist.
- **Routing is by sign-in, not a PIN.** Only people marked *Can route* see the Routing Desk, and every routing is kept.
- **An email is recorded as sent only when it really was.**
- **A front desk intake can be registered only once**, and a changed receipt time needs a reason.
- **Every reply and every response is listed** on the item, not just the first.
- **A holding reply doesn't close an inward item.**
- **Every change is recorded** with who made it and when (the History on each item).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Front desk officer | Log documents handed in; see the Awaiting registry list and their own intakes. No registers or files |
| Registry officer | Register and handle items, pull front desk intakes, review and resend acknowledgement emails, the Internal File Desk, slips and reports, dashboard |
| Head of Registry | As above, plus void items, correct any detail (with a reason), maintain types, lists and holidays, and Admin › Organisation |
| Unit officer | See the items routed to their unit and the letters their unit sent |
| Secretary / management | See everything; reports and dashboard |
| Administrator | Staff accounts, settings, and Admin › Organisation (units, divisions, email addresses, signatories, file class codes) |
| *Can route* (ticked on a person's account) | The Routing Desk, on top of their own role. Given to the Secretary to the Commission and the Head of Administration |
