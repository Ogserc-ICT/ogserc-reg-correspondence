# OGSERC Registry: guide for registry staff

The Registry system records everything the Commission receives (inward) or sends (outward). It issues acknowledgement slips, including those required by paragraph 10(3) of the OGSERC Order, routes items to the right unit, chases responses, and shows where every file is. It replaces the Registry Correspondence Workbook.

## Signing in

- Go to the Registry address your administrator gives you and sign in with your work email.
- The first time, you'll be asked to choose your own password: at least 12 characters, and a short sentence works well.
- After 5 wrong passwords your account locks for 15 minutes. An administrator can unlock it sooner.

## The Registry Desk

The home page shows today's figures:
- received today and dispatched today
- awaiting forwarding and incoming overdue
- not yet dispatched and replies overdue to us
- files out

Click a figure to see the items behind it.

From the Desk you can start **New inward item** or **New outward item**, and print a slip for any inward number.

## Logging an incoming item

1. **Record receipt.** On *New inward item*, enter the date and time received (it starts at now), the mode, the type, the sender and sender category, and the subject. You appear as "Received by". For updates, annual confirmations and deficiency responses, enter the sender's existing OGSERC number. If the item answers one of our letters, find our outward number under *In response to our ref.*: type part of the number or name and choose it.
2. **Note the contents.** For registration filings, count the Schedule 2 documents (12 required; 13 for a multi-State licensee), say whether fee evidence is attached, and record the payment reference and amount.
3. **Save.** Choose **Save to register**, **Save + print slip** or **Save + PDF slip**. The acknowledgement number is given when you save and never changes. The message tells you where to forward the item: *Saved as OGSERC/ACK/2026/00012. Forward to: Registration & Licensing.*
4. **Give the slip.** Print it, sign and stamp it, give the top copy to the sender and file the Registry copy. For email or portal items, send the PDF. Record how it went under *Acknowledgement issued via*.
5. **Route it.** On the item's page, press **Forwarded today** when it goes to the unit, or enter the date and who received it under *Update item*. Set the action officer and priority there too. The response due date follows the type's target (Urgent = 1 working day); use *Response due (override)* when the sender sets a deadline.
6. **Close it.** Log the Commission's reply as an outward item with this inward number under *In reply to*. The inward item closes when the reply is **dispatched**. A holding reply ("Acknowledgement / holding reply") doesn't close it. If the matter is settled another way, press **Close today** and write a short closing note.

If you leave out something required, the form lists every missing field under "Please complete:" and keeps what you typed.

## Logging an outgoing item

1. **Number it first.** Before the letter is signed, enter the date of the document, type, subject, addressee, recipient category, originating unit and signatory, and press **Save & get number**. Quote the number shown on the letter.
2. **Link it.** Choose the inward item it replies to (check that the sender shown is right) and the file.
3. **Dispatch it.** Press **Dispatched today** on the item's page, or enter the date, mode and tracking reference. When delivery is confirmed, press **Delivered today**. The system won't record a delivery before the dispatch.
4. **Follow up.** Where a reply is expected (deficiency notices, information requests, fee demands and so on), the response due date is set from the dispatch date. The item shows *Response received* as soon as the reply is logged as an inward item quoting this number.

## Files

On the File Desk (**Files**):
- **Issue a file:** open it and press **Issue file**, with who it goes to and why. If it's still out with someone else, you'll be asked to confirm before it's issued again.
- **Record a return:** press **Record return** when it comes back.
- **Add a new file:** use **Add to File Index**.

The File Index shows who has each file and for how long. Files overdue for return are shown in red.

## End of the day and the week

- **Daily Register:** print it at the end of each day, check the received and dispatched counts against the physical items and the dispatch book, and sign it with the Head of Registry. It lists items by the day they actually arrived. An email at 18:40 is on today's register even though it counts as received tomorrow.
- **Pending Actions:** review it with each unit weekly. It shows everything still open, overdue or awaiting forwarding; print it for all units or one.

## Numbers and dates

| | |
|---|---|
| Inward / Ack. No. | `OGSERC/ACK/YYYY/00001`. YYYY is the year of the deemed receipt date; the serial restarts each year |
| Outward No. | `OGSERC/OUT/YYYY/00001`. YYYY is the year of the document date |
| Deemed receipt (para 10(3)) | Email or portal items at or after the closing time (5:00 p.m.), and any item on a weekend or public holiday, count as received on the next working day. That date drives the registration deadline check and every response target |
| Targets | All in working days, skipping public holidays |

## Colours

The status is always written in words. The colour is a reminder:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red | Overdue |
| Amber | Awaiting action |
| Green | Closed / done |
| Peach | Flagged: something to check, listed on the item |

## What's different from the workbook

- **Numbers never change and nothing is deleted.** An item logged by mistake is *voided* by the Head of Registry with a reason. It stays on the register, struck through, and its number is never used again.
- **No more "Paste > Values".** Numbers are fixed the moment you save.
- **Links are chosen, not typed.** You can't link to a number that doesn't exist.
- **Every reply and every response is listed** on the item, not just the first.
- **A holding reply doesn't close an inward item.**
- **Every change is recorded** with who made it and when (the History on each item).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Registry officer | Log and handle items, File Desk, slips and reports, dashboard |
| Head of Registry | As above, plus void items, correct any detail (with a reason), and maintain types, lists and holidays |
| Unit officer | See the items routed to their unit and the letters their unit sent |
| Secretary / management | See everything; reports and dashboard |
| Administrator | Staff accounts and settings |
